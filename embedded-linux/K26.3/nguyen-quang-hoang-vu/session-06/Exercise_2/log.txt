=================================================
STEP 1 - Build Logger Service
=================================================

$ make

gcc -Wall -Wextra -std=c99 -O2 logger.c -o logger


=================================================
STEP 2 - Full Journal Logs
=================================================

$ journalctl -u logger

<6> INFO log 1
<4> WARNING log 1
<3> ERROR log 1
...
<6> INFO log 15
<4> WARNING log 15
<3> ERROR log 15


=================================================
STEP 3 - Warning and Above
=================================================

$ journalctl -p warning

<4> WARNING log 1
<3> ERROR log 1
...
<4> WARNING log 15
<3> ERROR log 15


=================================================
STEP 4 - Error and Above
=================================================

$ journalctl -p err

<3> ERROR log 1
...
<3> ERROR log 15

<3> Fatal error. Abort...


=================================================
STEP 5 - Last One Minute
=================================================

$ journalctl --since "1 minute ago"

<6> INFO log 14
<4> WARNING log 14
<3> ERROR log 14

<6> INFO log 15
<4> WARNING log 15
<3> ERROR log 15


=================================================
STEP 6 - Crash Point
=================================================

<3> Fatal error. Abort...

Program terminated by abort()


=================================================
OBSERVATION
=================================================

1. Logs are redirected to systemd journal.
2. INFO, WARNING and ERROR messages are recorded.
3. Service terminates by abort().
4. Crash information can be retrieved using journalctl.
