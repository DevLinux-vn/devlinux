==============================
STEP 1 - Build Service
==============================

$ make

gcc -Wall -Wextra -std=c99 -O2 monitor.c -o monitor


==============================
STEP 2 - Service Status
==============================

$ systemctl status monitor

(active service output)


==============================
STEP 3 - Send SIGTERM
==============================

$ kill -TERM <PID>

Service shutting down...


==============================
STEP 4 - Journal Logs
==============================

$ journalctl -u monitor

Monitor service started.
Service is running...
Service is running...
Service shutting down...


==============================
STEP 5 - Stop Service
==============================

$ systemctl stop monitor

Service stopped successfully.
